# Floor material photographs

These original 1024 by 1024 JPEG maps are bundled locally. The application does
not contact Poly Haven when a finish is selected. Downloaded 2026-10-04; each
file's MD5 was checked against the official Poly Haven files API.

All maps are dedicated to the public domain under [Poly Haven's CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license).

| Local maps | Original material | Author | Source dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `wood_floor_{diff,nor_gl,rough}_1k.jpg` | [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor) | Dimitrios Savva | 1.7 by 1.7 m |
| `floor_tiles_04_{diff,nor_gl,rough}_1k.jpg` | [Floor Tiles 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/floor_tiles_04) | Rob Tuytel | 4 by 4 m |

The wood photograph is tinted for Natural oak, Walnut, and Light oak vinyl;
the vinyl uses shallower normal detail. The stone photograph is tinted for
Soft stone and scaled to a 2.4 m repeating patch, giving 0.6 m tile courses.
Source photographs are unchanged. Diffuse maps are sRGB; OpenGL normal and
roughness maps are linear. All three maps rotate together in metre-based floor
UVs, independently of the captured room illumination. No displacement map is
used: these details do not move measured floor geometry.
